# ADR 0030: A repository's areas live in embry0; the area mapper proposes and a person applies

Status: accepted (2026-11-11)

A repository's areas (spec 2026-10-25) are a document embry0 keeps in `repo_areas`, not a file in the repository: the YAML as written and its checked values, which every reader reads, with a generation for `If-Match`. They are one more surface of the repository's history (`repo_config_revisions`, ADR 0019), so every write is recorded and the newest can be undone. They are their own document, not a settings layer (ADR 0028): a repository's parts are facts about it with no deployment or organization default. The area mapper never writes to the repository: its document waits in `repo_areas_proposals` until a person applies it, a compare-and-set against the areas the map started from, or rejects it. Writing areas and deciding a map take an operate key whose repository allowlist covers the repository, the tier that already sets a repository's owners. Globs follow qa.yaml's grammar, so one glob means the same in both documents; an area is the part people answer for, a qa.yaml app the thing that runs, and a knowledge map's `areas` are its scanner's groups. Area owners are logins, resolved with the repository's owners and `CODEOWNERS` in one place (`embry0/services/owners.py`). Each repository's old `.embry0/areas.yml` is imported once, for the repositories registered when migration 145 ran, and the next minor release removes the import.

Evidence: migration 145; `embry0/services/areas_document.py`, `embry0/services/areas_store.py`, `embry0/services/areas_proposals.py`, `embry0/services/areas_import.py`, `embry0/api/v1/areas.py`; [the design](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-areas-in-the-database-design.md); issues #159 and #175.
