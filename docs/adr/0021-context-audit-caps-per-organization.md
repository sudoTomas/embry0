# ADR 0021: An organization sets its own context audit caps, and the deployment's are the defaults

Status: accepted (2026-10-23); amended (2026-10-25): `embry0:context-audit` is exempt from the per-author rate limit, so the fleet budget caps and the per-organization caps bound the schedule, not that limit; amended (2026-10-30, #171): the decision stands, its storage moved. The two caps are the settings document's `budget.context_audit_job_usd` and `budget.context_audit_max_jobs_per_tick`, the organization's layer over the deployment's document (ADR 0028), read at every tick. Each organization's `context_audit_budget_usd` and `context_audit_max_jobs_per_tick`, and the two variables, were copied into them once; the static bound of 50 USD went, since every job's cap is bounded by the deployment's `budget.max_budget_per_job_usd` where it runs.

ADR 0020 capped the context audit schedule at `CONTEXT_AUDIT_MAX_JOBS_PER_TICK` jobs per tick across every organization, each job capped at `CONTEXT_AUDIT_JOB_BUDGET_USD`. This record corrects that clause. An organization's `context_audit_budget_usd` (more than 0 and at most 50 USD) and `context_audit_max_jobs_per_tick` (0 to 50) bound its own audits; they are written with `PATCH /orgs/{owner}` like its interval and read the same way, so a value a PATCH would refuse reads as unset, and unset the two variables apply. Each cap holds per tick for its own organization's repositories, so one organization's due repositories never wait behind another's, and a tick starts at most the sum of the caps. The per-job cap stays a hard stop; the fleet budget caps and the per-author rate limit on `embry0:context-audit` still bound the whole.

Evidence: `ContextAuditLoop.caps` in `embry0/services/context_audit.py` and the `budget` section in `embry0/settings/sections/budget.py`; before #171, `context_audit_budget_usd` and `context_audit_max_jobs_per_tick` in `embry0/services/organizations.py`, now its `MOVED_SETTINGS`.
